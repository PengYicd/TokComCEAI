Design a compact, stable, and efficient task-specific token communication protocol for two embodied agents.
$TASK_CONTEXT$

Requirements:
1. The protocol must contain `syntax rules`, `codebook`, and `example`.
2. Preserve coordination-critical meaning before compression. Different partner decisions must not collapse to the same encoded message.
3. Object and container codewords must be instance-specific: each codeword maps to exactly one `<name> (id)`. If two entities share a name but have different ids, they must use different codewords. Do not use generic type-level tokens such as `apple(any)`.
4. Action/state codewords may be reusable, but the protocol must clearly distinguish room claim/search, room empty/cleared, found items, taken items, remaining items, moving, and transporting/delivering.
5. Keep messages short and compositional. Messages are space-separated codewords, and each codeword must be a single short token without whitespace.
6. Include contrastive examples showing different encodings for: (a) same-name entities with different ids, and (b) different room states such as searching vs empty.
